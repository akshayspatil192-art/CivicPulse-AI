The 4-Stage Report Lifecycle

How CivicPulse transforms raw, unstructured noise into structured municipal resolution.

1. Capture

Citizens submit issues in under 60 seconds with text, images, GPS map pin, and automatic street address mapping.

2. Understand

AI automatically classifies the category, extracts a 1-sentence decision brief, flags duplicate reports, and scores hazard priority.

3. Reveal

Surface unseen patterns: compound risks (e.g. choked drain + waterlogging + road damage) and geographic hot clusters on interactive Leaflet maps.

4. Act

Transparent municipal workflow: auto-routed to departments (Roads, Drainage, Waste, Lighting), tracked by citizens via real-time timeline steppers.
